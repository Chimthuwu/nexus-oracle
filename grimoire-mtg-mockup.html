Rules tutor — live session
You
If I flash in a blocker after attackers are declared but before blocks, can it still block?
G
Yes — as long as it resolves before the declare blockers step begins, it's a legal blocker. Once blockers are declared, flashing in a new creature won't let it join that combat.
You
And if it has summoning sickness?
G
Doesn't matter for blocking — summoning sickness only stops attacking and tapping for abilities
Keyword glossary
Flying
Can only be blocked by creatures with flying or reach.
Trample
Excess combat damage beyond what's needed to kill blockers carries over to the defending player.
Flash
May be cast any time you could cast an instant, not just on your turn.
